I am Yipeng Sun (孙熠芃), a senior AI engineer at [NXAI](https://www.nx-ai.com) in Linz, Austria. I build embodied intelligence for robots. My PhD research at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg is about medical imaging and computed tomography reconstruction.

## 🛠️ What I build

- **[diffct](https://github.com/sypsyp97/diffct)** ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/diffct/)): Differentiable CT projectors for PyTorch, with multi-GPU operation and geometry gradients.
- **[Light-Whisper](https://github.com/sypsyp97/light-whisper)**: Speech-to-text for Windows. Hold a hotkey, speak, and the text goes into the active app.

### 🧰 Small tools that I build for every day use

- **[Kou](https://github.com/sypsyp97/kou)**: Local dictation for macOS. Kou does not send audio out of your Mac.
- **[lidwake](https://github.com/sypsyp97/lidwake)**: Prevents sleep on a MacBook with the lid closed while Claude Code is active.
- **[Sun Serif](https://github.com/sypsyp97/sun-serif)**: A serif font family for text and headings. My personal site uses it.

## 🦖 What I maintain

- **[TiRex](https://github.com/NX-AI/tirex)** ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/tirex-ts/)) and **[TiRex-2](https://github.com/NX-AI/tirex-2)** ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/tirex-2/)): NXAI models for zero-shot forecasts of time series.
